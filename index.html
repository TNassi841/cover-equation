Why this is on the cover

Breathing is regulated by feedback that arrives late: the brain senses blood gases some time after each breath. When that feedback is too strong or too slow, breathing can swing instead of settling. This thesis measures that instability from routine sleep recordings.

About this figure

The artwork uses the Mackey–Glass equation, a classic model of delayed physiological feedback (Science, 1977). It is an illustration, separate from the model in the thesis, and the shapes carry no clinical meaning.
